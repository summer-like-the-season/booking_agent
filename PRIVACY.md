# Privacy Policy: Booking Agent

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Booking Agent is a personal tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

## What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses these Google permissions:

- **Google Calendar events** (`calendar.events`): to read the owner's events, and to add guests to appointments the owner has booked.
- **Free/busy information** (`calendar.freebusy`): to avoid booking times when the owner is already busy.
- **Send email** (`gmail.send`): to send the owner a notification after each booking. The app cannot read, search or delete any email.

## What the app does with it

- Calendar data is used only while the app is running, to choose and confirm appointment times.
- To decide which time slot to book, booking-page content and calendar times are sent to the Anthropic API (Claude). No other third party receives the data.
- The only data stored is on the owner's computer: a Google sign-in token and a list of bookings the app has made.
- Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

## Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>, and delete the local files to remove everything the app has stored.
